Our story

Kokopo roots. PNG colour. Maker-proud.

Kokopo beginnings

Lavagirl is based in Kokopo, East New Britain. Led by Annette Sete, we design fabric, jewellery, and meri blouses that carry PNG motifs with care and respect.

Sample text: We started small, working with local makers and selling at markets. Today we continue to make everything by hand, in small batches.

The meaning of tabu

Inspired by Tolai shell-money culture. The tabu ring is a symbol we hold with respect. We do not claim it belongs to anyone; we credit it as an inspiration and design with care.

Sample text: Our Plain Tabu design repeats the ring motif in six colourways, each piece made to celebrate PNG's visual heritage while keeping the interpretation respectful.

The makers

Sample text: Our small team of makers in Kokopo cut, sew, and bead each piece. We work closely with local artisans and pay fair rates for their work.

Why authentic PNG-made matters

Sample text: Authentic PNG-made fashion supports local livelihoods and keeps traditional motifs in the hands of the communities they come from. We believe in transparency and respect for cultural heritage.

Cultural care note: The tabu motif is credited as inspired by Tolai shell-money culture. The owner must approve all wording and pattern accuracy before publishing.
