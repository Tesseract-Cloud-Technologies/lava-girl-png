STORY · KOKOPO

Made by hand, led by heritage.

Lavagirl is based in Kokopo, East New Britain. Led by Annette Sete, we design fabric, jewellery, and meri blouses that carry PNG motifs with care and respect.

Inspired by Tolai shell-money culture. We honour the tabu, but do not claim it belongs to anyone.
